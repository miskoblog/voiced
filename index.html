How Voiced works

MagicFlixAI 2.0's AI Channel Brain trains on whatever brand voice you give it. Voiced helps you build that voice — a specific, differentiated persona — before you sit down to generate your first video.

–Before you start

Voiced doesn't connect to MagicFlixAI 2.0 or generate any video — it prepares the words you paste into MagicFlixAI's Brand Voice Training step when you set up a channel there.

1Build a persona brief

Open the Persona Builder tab.

  1. Pick your channel's Channel Theme and Platform.
  2. Choose a Tone and a Narrator / Host type.
  3. No need to write any of the remaining fields yourself — Character Name & Short Bio, Signature Motif, Catchphrase, Target Viewer, and Avoid / Off-Limits each have a small Pick an idea dropdown right above them. Pick one to fill the field with a ready-made line, then leave it as-is or change a word or two to make it more yours.
  4. Press Generate Brief. Your Channel Brain Brief appears on the right.
  5. Press Copy to paste the brief straight into MagicFlixAI's Brand Voice Training step, or press Save to keep it.

2Browse more ideas in the Voice & Motif Bank

Want more options than the dropdowns show? Open the Voice & Motif Bank tab to browse every catchphrase, narrator archetype, and motif for your theme in one place — the same ideas, just easier to scan side by side.

3Check your Differentiation Score

Every time you press Generate Brief, open the Differentiation Score tab to see a 0–100 score for how generic vs. differentiated the current persona is, plus specific flags on which fields are still too vague.

4Reopen a Saved Persona

Open the Saved Personas tab to see everything you've saved. Press Open on any entry to reload its exact inputs back into the Persona Builder so you can tweak and regenerate, or Delete ones you no longer need.

–One thing to know

Voiced is fully static and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — your in-progress persona and everything in your Saved Personas list live only in this browser's local storage, on this device. Clearing your browser data, or opening Voiced on a different device, starts fresh.
